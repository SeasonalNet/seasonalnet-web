These policies describe the conditions under which SeasonalNet-operated services may be used and how SeasonalNet processes information required to operate and protect those services.

SeasonalNet is a privately operated homelab platform provided at the operator's expense. It is not an advertising platform, data broker, or commercial analytics service.

## Policy documents

- [Privacy Notice](/docs/policies/privacy) — what information SeasonalNet processes, why it is processed, and how it is handled
- [Terms of Service](/docs/policies/terms) — the general conditions for accessing or using SeasonalNet-operated services
- [Acceptable Use Policy](/docs/policies/acceptable-use) — prohibited activity and the security controls SeasonalNet may apply

## Scope

These policies apply to SeasonalNet-operated websites, APIs, account systems, provisioning systems, PBX services, VPN access, administrative interfaces, and related infrastructure where SeasonalNet determines how the service is operated.

They do not replace the separate rules or policies of third-party platforms, external communities, software providers, network operators, or services merely linked from SeasonalNet.

## Relationship between the policies

The Privacy Notice explains information handling. The Terms of Service establish the general service conditions. The Acceptable Use Policy is incorporated into the Terms of Service and defines conduct that may result in blocking, suspension, credential revocation, or termination.

Questions about these policies may be sent to `info@seasonalnet.org`.
