## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy protects SeasonalNet systems, users, upstream providers, and other networks from misuse. It applies to all access to SeasonalNet-operated services and is incorporated into the [Terms of Service](/docs/policies/terms).

Ordinary browsing, authorized account use, documented API use, and reasonable automation within published or assigned limits are permitted. Activity must remain within the authorization actually granted.

## 2. Prohibited activity

You may not use SeasonalNet services to perform, facilitate, conceal, or materially assist the following activity.

### Unauthorized access and security abuse

- Accessing or attempting to access systems, accounts, data, networks, extensions, credentials, administrative functions, or service resources without authorization
- Bypassing or attempting to bypass authentication, authorization, multifactor authentication, network segmentation, rate limits, firewall rules, bans, quarantines, or other security controls
- Exploiting or attempting to exploit vulnerabilities without explicit prior authorization
- Performing port scans, vulnerability scans, credential attacks, fuzzing, enumeration, or similar testing against SeasonalNet or systems reached through SeasonalNet without explicit prior authorization
- Intercepting, modifying, replaying, or forging traffic, credentials, tokens, certificates, sessions, caller identity, or service responses without authorization
- Introducing malware, destructive payloads, unauthorized remote-control mechanisms, or persistence

### Availability and resource abuse

- Denial-of-service activity or deliberate service degradation
- Excessive automated requests, connection floods, resource exhaustion, or behavior intended to evade normal limits
- Using SeasonalNet as a relay, proxy, tunnel, scanner, bot, command-and-control system, or attack origin without explicit authorization
- Interfering with monitoring, logging, backups, incident response, or administrative controls

### Identity, credential, and account abuse

- Sharing, selling, transferring, publishing, or misusing credentials or enrolled devices
- Impersonating another user, administrator, service, organization, or network
- Using compromised credentials or continuing to use access known to be unauthorized
- Creating or operating accounts, devices, or sessions to evade a restriction or prior enforcement action

### Communications and PBX abuse

- Spam, unsolicited bulk communications, harassment, threats, fraud, phishing, or deceptive communications
- Toll fraud, unauthorized premium or chargeable calling, abusive robocalling, or deliberate call flooding
- Unauthorized caller-ID manipulation, extension impersonation, interception, recording, or disclosure of communications
- Using SeasonalNet communications services in violation of applicable law or the rights of another person

### Content and legal abuse

- Uploading, transmitting, or distributing material that is unlawful, malicious, intentionally deceptive, or infringes another person's rights
- Using SeasonalNet to facilitate fraud, theft, unauthorized surveillance, or other unlawful activity
- Publishing secrets, credentials, private keys, access tokens, or personal information without authorization

## 3. Security research and vulnerability reports

SeasonalNet does not grant permission for unsolicited active security testing.

If you discover a suspected vulnerability during ordinary use:

1. Stop testing once the issue is reasonably demonstrated.
2. Do not access, change, retain, or disclose data that is not yours.
3. Do not disrupt the service or attempt persistence.
4. Report the issue by sending it to the SeasonalNet Security Mailbox at `security@seasonalnet.org` with enough detail to reproduce and investigate it safely.

Further testing requires explicit written authorization from SeasonalNet staff.

## 4. Enforcement

SeasonalNet may use automated and manual controls to enforce this policy, including request rejection, rate limiting, firewall or reverse-proxy blocks, account restrictions, credential revocation, device quarantine, service suspension, and termination.

SeasonalNet may preserve and review relevant request, network, account, credential, device, application, and audit records to investigate suspected violations and prevent repeated abuse. Enforcement may occur without advance notice where immediate action is reasonably necessary to protect the platform or another system.

Where appropriate, SeasonalNet may notify an upstream provider, affected operator, or lawful authority of serious abuse. SeasonalNet is not required to provide continued access, disclose internal detection methods, or remove a protective control while the underlying risk remains.

## 5. Reporting abuse

Reports concerning abuse of SeasonalNet-operated services may be sent to the SeasonalNet Operator at `seasonal@seasonalnet.org`. Include relevant timestamps, hostnames, source or destination information, and a concise description of the activity where available.
