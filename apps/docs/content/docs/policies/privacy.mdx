## 1. Overview

SeasonalNet processes information only where it is reasonably necessary to provide requested services, authenticate and authorize users, maintain service state, diagnose faults, protect the platform, and enforce platform security.

SeasonalNet does not sell personal information, operate an advertising network, or intentionally build behavioral profiles for unrelated marketing purposes.

## 2. Scope

This notice applies to SeasonalNet-operated websites, APIs, account systems, provisioning systems, PBX services, VPN access, administrative interfaces, and related infrastructure where SeasonalNet determines how information is processed.

This notice does not govern third-party services merely linked from SeasonalNet or independently operated services that publish their own privacy terms.

## 3. Information SeasonalNet may process

Depending on the service used, SeasonalNet may process the following categories of information.

### Network and request information

- Source IP address and network connection information
- Request date and time
- Requested hostname, path, method, response status, and transferred data size
- Browser, client, or user-agent information
- Referrer information when supplied by the client
- Reverse-proxy, rate-limit, firewall, and abuse-detection events
- Security signals supplied by infrastructure providers such as Cloudflare

### Account and authentication information

- Account identifiers, usernames, display names, and email addresses
- Identity-provider subject identifiers and linked account identifiers
- Group memberships, roles, and authorization state
- Login, logout, session, multifactor-authentication, and access-control events
- Device or credential identifiers needed to provide an authenticated service

SeasonalNet does not need or receive a user's external identity-provider password when authentication is delegated through a supported identity provider.

### Service and operational records

- Service enrollment, ownership, configuration, and status records
- PBX extension ownership, provisioning, credential-rotation, and related operation records
- VPN device, enrollment, access, and policy-enforcement records
- API requests, administrative actions, and audit records
- Content or files intentionally submitted to a service, including chat input, provisioning data, or administrative media uploads
- Fault reports, support messages, and other correspondence sent to SeasonalNet

### Browser storage

SeasonalNet applications may use cookies, session storage, or local storage for necessary authentication, security, user-interface state, and service continuity. SeasonalNet does not use this storage for third-party advertising profiles.

## 4. Why information is processed

SeasonalNet may process the information described above to:

- Deliver requested pages, APIs, streams, communications, and account-backed services
- Authenticate users and determine whether they are authorized to perform an action
- Maintain account, device, provisioning, PBX, VPN, and service state
- Operate, monitor, troubleshoot, back up, and improve service reliability
- Detect scanning, exploitation attempts, credential abuse, spam, denial-of-service activity, evasion, and other misuse
- Apply rate limits, firewall rules, temporary or persistent blocks, account restrictions, suspensions, credential revocation, or other protective controls
- Investigate incidents, preserve platform integrity, and protect SeasonalNet systems and users
- Comply with valid legal obligations or respond to lawful requests where applicable

## 5. Security logging and enforcement

SeasonalNet uses web-server logs, reverse-proxy logs, authentication records, application audit records, firewall events, and automated abuse-prevention systems to protect the platform.

This processing may include associating a request, network address, account, credential, or device with suspicious or prohibited activity. SeasonalNet may retain and use those records to investigate the event, prevent repeated abuse, and enforce the [Acceptable Use Policy](/docs/policies/acceptable-use).

Automated controls may reject requests, rate-limit traffic, or block a network address before a human review occurs. Immediate protective action may be taken where activity presents a security or availability risk.

## 6. Infrastructure and service providers

SeasonalNet may rely on infrastructure and software providers to deliver services. For example, Cloudflare may process request and security information while providing authoritative DNS, reverse proxying, transport security, availability, and abuse mitigation for public SeasonalNet services.

Other providers or independently maintained software components may process information only as required for the service in which they participate. Their own terms and privacy notices may also apply when they independently determine how information is handled.

## 7. Disclosure of information

SeasonalNet does not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed only where reasonably necessary to:

- Operate a service through an infrastructure or service provider
- Investigate or remediate security incidents and abuse
- Protect SeasonalNet, its users, or other systems from harm
- Comply with a valid legal obligation or lawful request
- Obtain technical, administrative, or legal assistance where confidentiality is appropriate

## 8. Retention

SeasonalNet retains information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

Retention periods vary by service and record type. Routine request logs may be rotated on an operational schedule. Account, ownership, provisioning, audit, backup, and security-enforcement records may be retained longer where needed to maintain service state, investigate incidents, prevent repeated abuse, restore systems, or document administrative actions.

Information that is no longer operationally or legally necessary may be deleted, expired, rotated, or anonymized. Backup copies may persist until the applicable backup cycle expires.

## 9. User choices and requests

Users may choose not to provide optional information, but some SeasonalNet services cannot function without the identifiers, credentials, request data, or service records required to provide and secure them.

Questions or reasonable requests concerning information associated with a SeasonalNet account may be sent to the SeasonalNet Head Administrator at `seasonal@seasonalnet.org`. SeasonalNet may require enough information to verify the requester's identity and authority before disclosing, changing, or deleting account-related records.

Some records may need to be retained despite a deletion request where they remain necessary for security, abuse prevention, service integrity, backup expiration, or a legal obligation.

## 10. Changes to this notice

SeasonalNet may update this notice when services, security controls, or information-handling practices materially change. The effective and last-updated dates at the top of this page identify the current version.
