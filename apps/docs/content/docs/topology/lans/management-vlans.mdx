## VLAN Family

SeasonalNet reserves VLANs `40` through `49` for management and control-plane networks. VLANs 40 through 43 are allocated; VLANs 44 through 49 remain reserved for future management functions.

| VLAN | Name | IPv4 | IPv6 ULA | Gateway | Current state |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 40 | `NET-MGMT` | `10.40.0.0/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:40::/64` | `10.40.0.1` / `fd97:418:22dd:40::1` | Routed and available; general network-device management migration is scheduled |
| 41 | `BMC-MGMT` | `10.41.0.0/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:41::/64` | `10.41.0.1` / `fd97:418:22dd:41::1` | Active; PowerEdge iDRAC interfaces migrated here |
| 42 | `CME-MGMT` | `10.42.0.0/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:42::/64` | `10.42.0.1` / `fd97:418:22dd:42::1` | Active; CME management plus transitional network-device management |
| 43 | `HOST-MGMT` | `10.43.0.0/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:43::/64` | `10.43.0.1` / `fd97:418:22dd:43::1` | Routed and reserved for host/hypervisor management; no production host cutover yet |
| 44-49 | Reserved | — | — | — | Held for future management/control-plane separation |

The fourth ULA hextet follows the printed VLAN number, consistent with the site-wide `fd97:418:22dd::/48` allocation.

## Layer-3 Ownership

SeasonalNet-DSW1 owns the IPv4 and IPv6 gateway SVIs for VLANs 40 through 43. The new VLAN 40, 41, and 43 SVIs are routed into the same DSW1/FW4C topology as the existing routed VLANs and are passive OSPF interfaces: their connected prefixes are advertised without forming neighbor adjacencies on the management LANs.

| SVI | IPv4 | IPv6 | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Vlan40` | `10.40.0.1/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:40::1/64` | NET-MGMT gateway |
| `Vlan41` | `10.41.0.1/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:41::1/64` | BMC-MGMT gateway |
| `Vlan42` | `10.42.0.1/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:42::1/64` | CME-MGMT gateway and current transitional switch-management gateway |
| `Vlan43` | `10.43.0.1/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:43::1/64` | HOST-MGMT gateway |

Main-LAN IPv4 return routes for VLANs 40, 41, 42, and 43 point through FW4C at `192.168.1.5`. FW4C learns the routed management prefixes from DSW1 over OSPF.

## BMC-MGMT

The two production PowerEdge BMCs were migrated from Main LAN to VLAN 41 on 2026-08-11.

| Address | Device | Attachment | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `10.41.0.32` | SeasonalR230-iDRAC | SW2 `Gi1/0/13`, access VLAN 41 | Dell PowerEdge R230 iDRAC8 |
| `10.41.0.33` | SeasonalR340-iDRAC | SW2 `Gi1/0/15`, access VLAN 41 | Dell PowerEdge R340 iDRAC9 |

The corresponding Main-LAN addresses `192.168.1.32` and `192.168.1.33` are free again. The Proxmox host interfaces remain on Main LAN at `192.168.1.30` and `192.168.1.31`.

The BMCs currently use IPv4 only. The VLAN 41 ULA and DSW1 IPv6 gateway are allocated and routed, but iDRAC IPv6 is disabled until deliberately enabled and documented.

FW4C policy permits the required administrative path and a narrow HTTPS/Redfish monitoring path into BMC-MGMT rather than treating the VLAN as generally reachable from Main LAN.

## NET-MGMT Transition

VLAN 40 is the target management network for switches and other general network infrastructure. The network-device management identities have **not** been renumbered yet.

Current transitional assignments still on CME-MGMT/VLAN 42:

| Current address | Device | Planned state |
|---|---|---|
| `10.42.0.1` / `fd97:418:22dd:42::1` | SeasonalNet-DSW1 | Move general network-management identity to VLAN 40 |
| `10.42.0.2` | SeasonalNet-SW1 | Move management SVI to VLAN 40 |
| `10.42.0.3` / `fd97:418:22dd:42::3` | SeasonalNet-SW2 | Move management SVI to VLAN 40 |
| `10.42.0.4` / `fd97:418:22dd:42::4` | SeasonalNet-SW3 | Move management SVI to VLAN 40 |

Until that cutover is performed, documentation and monitoring must continue to use the live VLAN 42 switch addresses. New general network-device management assignments should target NET-MGMT rather than extending the transitional VLAN 42 pattern.

After the migration, VLAN 42 remains allocated as `CME-MGMT` for CME-specific management functions.

## Layer-2 Distribution

VLANs 40, 41, and 43 were added to the existing management-capable trunks alongside VLAN 42. DSW1 remains the Rapid-PVST root for the 4x VLAN family.

| Link | Management VLANs carried | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DSW1 `Gi1/0/12` ↔ SW1 `Gi0/25` | 40, 41, 42, 43 | Direct fiber root path |
| DSW1 `Gi1/0/11` ↔ SW2 `Gi1/0/25` | 40, 41, 42, 43 | Direct fiber root path |
| DSW1 `Gi1/0/10` ↔ SW3 `Gi0/11` | 40, 41, 42, 43 | Direct fiber root path |
| SW1 `Gi0/23` ↔ SW2 `Gi1/0/24` | 40, 41, 42, 43 | Retained redundant copper trunk |
| SW2 `Gi1/0/22` ↔ SW3 `Gi0/10` | 40, 41, 42, 43 | Retained redundant copper trunk |

The existing voice, Phone-LAN, and Main-LAN VLAN membership on these trunks remains documented in the corresponding segment maps.

## DNS and Monitoring

SeasonalDNS owns the internal BMC records:

- `r230-idrac.lan.seasonalnet.org` and `seasonalr230-idrac.lan.seasonalnet.org` resolve to `10.41.0.32`.
- `r340-idrac.lan.seasonalnet.org` and `seasonalr340-idrac.lan.seasonalnet.org` resolve to `10.41.0.33`.

SeasonalMonitor reaches the BMCs over HTTPS/Redfish through the restricted firewall path. Monitoring targets use the internal FQDNs rather than embedding the BMC addresses.

The switch DNS records continue to resolve to their current VLAN 42 addresses until the NET-MGMT cutover is performed.
