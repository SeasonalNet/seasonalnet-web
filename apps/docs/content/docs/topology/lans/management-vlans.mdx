## VLAN Family

SeasonalNet reserves VLANs `40` through `49` for management and control-plane networks. VLANs 40 through 43 are allocated; VLANs 44 through 49 remain reserved for future management functions.

| VLAN | Name | IPv4 | IPv6 ULA | Gateway | Current state |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 40 | `NET-MGMT` | `10.40.0.0/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:40::/64` | `10.40.0.1` / `fd97:418:22dd:40::1` | Active general network-device management network |
| 41 | `BMC-MGMT` | `10.41.0.0/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:41::/64` | `10.41.0.1` / `fd97:418:22dd:41::1` | Active PowerEdge iDRAC management network |
| 42 | `CME-MGMT` | `10.42.0.0/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:42::/64` | `10.42.0.1` / `fd97:418:22dd:42::1` | Active CME-specific management network |
| 43 | `HOST-MGMT` | `10.43.0.0/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:43::/64` | `10.43.0.1` / `fd97:418:22dd:43::1` | Routed and reserved for host/hypervisor management; no production host cutover yet |
| 44-49 | Reserved | — | — | — | Held for future management/control-plane separation |

The fourth ULA hextet follows the printed VLAN number, consistent with the site-wide `fd97:418:22dd::/48` allocation.

## Layer-3 Ownership

SeasonalNet-DSW1 owns the IPv4 and IPv6 gateway SVIs for all VLANs except Main-LAN. These management-family SVIs are routed into the DSW1/FW4C topology and are passive OSPF interfaces.

| SVI | IPv4 | IPv6 | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Vlan40` | `10.40.0.1/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:40::1/64` | NET-MGMT gateway and DSW1 management identity |
| `Vlan41` | `10.41.0.1/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:41::1/64` | BMC-MGMT gateway |
| `Vlan42` | `10.42.0.1/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:42::1/64` | CME-MGMT gateway |
| `Vlan43` | `10.43.0.1/24` | `fd97:418:22dd:43::1/64` | HOST-MGMT gateway |

Main-LAN IPv4 return routes for VLANs 40, 41, 42, and 43 point through FW4C at `192.168.1.5`. FW4C learns the routed management prefixes from DSW1 over OSPF.

## NET-MGMT

The network-device management migration to VLAN 40 was completed on 2026-09-12. General switch and autonomous-AP management no longer uses CME-MGMT/VLAN 42.

| Address | Device | Management interface | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `10.40.0.1` / `fd97:418:22dd:40::1` | SeasonalNet-DSW1 | `Vlan40` | NET-MGMT gateway and distribution-switch management identity |
| `10.40.0.2` / `fd97:418:22dd:40::2` | SeasonalNet-ASW1 | `Vlan40` | Catalyst 3850 access-switch management |
| `10.40.0.3` / `fd97:418:22dd:40::3` | SeasonalNet-SW3 | `Vlan40` | Compact Phone-LAN/access-switch management |
| `10.40.0.4` / `fd97:418:22dd:40::4` | SeasonalAP-3602i | `BVI1` | Autonomous AP management; Voice VLAN 10 remains a separate service VLAN |
| `10.40.0.5` / `fd97:418:22dd:40::5` | SeasonalAP-2702E | `BVI1` | Autonomous AP management; Phone-LAN VLAN 30 remains a separate service VLAN |

Static network-device management should continue the NET-MGMT allocation rather than placing general infrastructure identities on CME-MGMT.

## CME-MGMT

CME-MGMT remains allocated specifically for Cisco CME management after the general network-device migration.

| Address | Device | Interface | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `10.42.0.1` / `fd97:418:22dd:42::1` | SeasonalNet-DSW1 | `Vlan42` | Routed CME-MGMT gateway only; DSW1 general management uses VLAN 40 |
| `10.42.0.2` / `fd97:418:22dd:42::2` | SeasonalCME-R1 | `Gi0/0.42` | Tagged CME management subinterface |

SeasonalCME-R1 keeps its call-control/service identity on Voice VLAN 10 at `10.10.0.10` / `fd97:418:22dd:10::10`.

## BMC-MGMT

The two production PowerEdge BMCs were migrated from Main LAN to VLAN 41 on 2026-08-11.

| Address | Device | Attachment | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `10.41.0.32` | SeasonalR230-iDRAC | ASW1 `Gi1/0/10`, access VLAN 41 | Dell PowerEdge R230 iDRAC8 |
| `10.41.0.33` | SeasonalR340-iDRAC | ASW1 `Gi1/0/12`, access VLAN 41 | Dell PowerEdge R340 iDRAC9 |

The corresponding Main-LAN addresses `192.168.1.32` and `192.168.1.33` are free again. The Proxmox host interfaces remain on Main LAN at `192.168.1.30` and `192.168.1.31`.

The BMCs currently use IPv4 only. The VLAN 41 ULA and DSW1 IPv6 gateway are allocated and routed, but iDRAC IPv6 is disabled until deliberately enabled and documented.

FW4C policy permits the required administrative path and a narrow HTTPS/Redfish monitoring path into BMC-MGMT rather than treating the VLAN as generally reachable from Main LAN.

## Layer-2 Distribution

DSW1 remains the Rapid-PVST root for the management VLAN family. ASW1 uses a two-member LACP trunk to DSW1; SW3 retains a direct fiber trunk to DSW1 plus a redundant trunk to ASW1.

| Link | VLANs carried | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DSW1 `Po1` (`Gi1/0/11-12`) ↔ ASW1 `Po1` (`Gi1/1/1-2`) | 10, 30, 40, 41, 42, 43, 99 | LACP trunk; native VLAN 99 |
| DSW1 `Gi1/0/10` ↔ SW3 `Gi0/11` | 30, 40, 41, 42, 43, 99 | Direct fiber trunk; native VLAN 99 |
| ASW1 `Gi1/0/3` ↔ SW3 `Gi0/10` | 30, 40, 41, 42, 43, 99 | Redundant copper trunk; native VLAN 99 and controlled by Rapid-PVST |
| ASW1 `Gi1/0/4` ↔ SeasonalAP-3602i `Gi0` | 10, 40 | AP trunk; native management VLAN 40, tagged Voice VLAN 10 |
| SW3 `Gi0/2` ↔ SeasonalAP-2702E `Gi0` | 30, 40 | AP trunk; native management VLAN 40, tagged Phone-LAN VLAN 30 |
| ASW1 `Gi1/0/5` ↔ SeasonalCME-R1 `Gi0/0` | 10, 42 | CME trunk; native Voice VLAN 10, tagged CME-MGMT VLAN 42 |

## PKI Management-Plane Sources

Legacy Cisco infrastructure uses the SeasonalCA RSA/SCEP path.

| Device | SCEP source | RSA identity-key policy |
|---|---|---|
| SeasonalNet-DSW1 | `Vlan40` | Existing device RSA keypair |
| SeasonalNet-ASW1 | `Vlan40` | Existing device RSA keypair |
| SeasonalNet-SW3 | `Vlan40` | Dedicated device RSA keypair |
| SeasonalAP-3602i | `BVI1` | Dedicated `SeasonalAP-3602i-SCEP-RSA` keypair |
| SeasonalAP-2702E | `BVI1` | Dedicated `SeasonalAP-2702E-SCEP-RSA` keypair |
| SeasonalCME-R1 | `Gi0/0.42` | Dedicated `SeasonalCME-R1-SCEP-RSA` keypair |

The dedicated SCEP-RSA labels on CME-R1 and the Aironet APs avoid collisions with separately maintained EC device keys.
