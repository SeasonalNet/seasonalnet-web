## Purpose

SeasonalVPN is the Defguard/WireGuard remote-access and administration plane for SeasonalNet. It provides authenticated routed access to permitted internal networks without making the VPN overlay an unrestricted Layer-2 extension of Main LAN.

---

## Address Bands

| Range / Prefix | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `10.88.0.1` | SeasonalVPN IPv4 gateway on `wg0` |
| `10.88.0.2 – 10.88.0.254` | Defguard-assigned VPN client addresses |
| `fd97:418:22dd:88::1` | SeasonalVPN IPv6 ULA gateway on `wg0` |
| `fd97:418:22dd:88::2 – fd97:418:22dd:88:ffff:ffff:ffff:ffff` | VPN client ULA assignments |

Defguard may assign host routes such as `/32` and `/128` to individual clients even though the routed overlay is documented as a `/24` and `/64`.

## Components

| Component | Role |
|---|---|
| Defguard core | User, device, network, and MFA policy management |

## Routing

| Route / relationship | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `10.88.0.0/24` via `192.168.1.22` | WNC-CR200A | Main-LAN IPv4 return route; metric `10` |
| `fd97:418:22dd:88::/64` via SeasonalVPN Main-LAN link-local | WNC-CR200A | Main-LAN IPv6 ULA return route; metric `10` |
| `10.88.0.0/24` via `192.168.1.22` | SeasonalEDGE-R1 | Static route redistributed into OSPFv2 under the VPN-client route policy |
| Internal SeasonalNet prefixes | SeasonalVPN / upstream routing policy | Routed access is permitted according to Defguard and host/router policy |

The VPN ULA is private routed space. It is not advertised to the public Internet and is not translated into a public IPv6 prefix.

---

## Security Boundary

- WireGuard transport alone is not treated as sufficient authorization when Defguard quarantine/MFA policy applies.
- VPN clients receive routed access, not automatic membership in every LAN broadcast domain.
- Per-client tunnel addresses preserve client attribution better than a generic SSH bastion or shared NAT identity.
- Main-LAN, SeasonalCME, Phone-LAN, and service access remain subject to routing and firewall policy.
