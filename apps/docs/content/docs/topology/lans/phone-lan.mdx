## Spanning Tree

SeasonalNet-DSW1 is the Rapid-PVST root for every VLAN carried by SW3. The direct fiber trunk is the preferred path, while the retained copper trunk to SW2 remains connected as a redundant Layer-2 path.

| VLAN(s) | SW3 port / role | Peer | Operational state |
|---|---|---|---|
| `30`, `42`, `99` | `Gi0/11` — root port | DSW1 `Gi1/0/10` | Forwarding; direct fiber path to the root bridge |
| `30`, `42`, `99` | `Gi0/10` — alternate port | SW2 `Gi1/0/22` | Blocking/discarding on SW3; SW2 is designated/forwarding on the segment |

The copper trunk is not unused or administratively disabled. Rapid-PVST keeps it out of the forwarding topology during normal operation and can reconverge through it if the preferred fiber path becomes unavailable.

## Notes and Constraints

- Phone-LAN moved from `192.168.56.0/24` / `fd42:56::/64` to `10.30.0.0/24` / `fd97:418:22dd:30::/64`.
- SeasonalPBX remains the active PBX service but does not route Phone-LAN.
- SeasonalNet-SW3 replaced the temporary unmanaged PoE switch and provides VLAN-aware distribution.
- Main-LAN native GUA addresses are live ISP state and are not stable Phone-LAN identity.
