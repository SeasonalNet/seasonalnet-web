## Spanning Tree

SeasonalNet-DSW1 remains the Rapid-PVST root for every VLAN carried by SW3. The direct fiber trunk on `Gi0/11` is the preferred DSW1 path; `Gi0/10` provides a redundant path through ASW1 and remains under Rapid-PVST control.

| VLAN(s) | SW3 port | Peer | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `30`, `40`, `41`, `42`, `43`, `99` | `Gi0/11` | DSW1 `Gi1/0/10` | Direct fiber path to the root bridge |
| `30`, `40`, `41`, `42`, `43`, `99` | `Gi0/10` | ASW1 `Gi1/0/3` | Redundant trunk; forwarding state is controlled by Rapid-PVST |

## Notes and Constraints

- Phone-LAN moved from `192.168.56.0/24` / `fd42:56::/64` to `10.30.0.0/24` / `fd97:418:22dd:30::/64`.
- SeasonalPBX remains the active PBX service but does not route Phone-LAN.
- SeasonalNet-SW3 replaced the temporary unmanaged PoE switch and provides VLAN-aware distribution.
- SW3 and SeasonalAP-2702E management use NET-MGMT/VLAN 40; Phone-LAN VLAN 30 is a service network rather than their management plane.
- Main-LAN native GUA addresses are live ISP state and are not stable Phone-LAN identity.
