The former Voice VLAN 10 IPv4 subnet `10.42.0.0/24` was moved to VLAN 42, and Voice VLAN 10 was renumbered to `10.10.0.0/24`. VLAN 42 is now named `CME-MGMT`. General switch/network-device management remains there only as a transition and is scheduled to move to VLAN 40 / `NET-MGMT`. The former management subnet `10.42.2.0/24` and legacy `fd42:42::/64` / `fd42:42:2::/64` prefixes are retired. The complete VLAN 40–43 management-family state is documented in [`management-vlans-map.md`](/docs/topology/lans/management-vlans).

## Address Bands

### Voice VLAN 10 (`10.10.0.0/24`)

| Range | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `.1` | DSW1 gateway SVI |
| `.2 – .9` | Network infrastructure and reserved addresses |
| `.10 – .19` | Core voice infrastructure |
| `.20 – .99` | Reserved static voice devices / important endpoints |
| `.100 – .254` | DHCP pool / dynamic clients |

Fixed assignments include `10.10.0.1` (SeasonalNet-DSW1), `10.10.0.4` (SeasonalAP-3602i), and `10.10.0.10` (SeasonalCME-R1).

### CME-MGMT VLAN 42 (`10.42.0.0/24`)

| Range | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `.1` | DSW1 gateway / current transitional management identity |
| `.2 – .19` | CME management plus transitional network-device management |
| `.20 – .99` | Reserved static managed devices / expansion |
| `.100 – .254` | Reserved for future use |

CME-MGMT VLAN 42 uses static infrastructure addressing. It does not provide a general client DHCPv4 pool. Existing DSW1/SW1/SW2/SW3 management identities remain here until the scheduled NET-MGMT/VLAN 40 cutover.

## Distribution, Switching, and Uplinks

| Device | Role | Key uplink(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| SeasonalNet-DSW1 (`WS-C3850-12S`) | Layer-3 distribution core and STP root | `Gi1/0/1` to FW4C; `Gi1/0/10` to SW3; `Gi1/0/11` to SW2; `Gi1/0/12` to SW1 | Owns VLAN 10/30/40/41/42/43 SVIs, inter-VLAN routing, routed default path, Rapid-PVST root, and routed-VLAN IGMP querier roles where configured, running IP Services |
| FW4C | Production edge firewall/router | Main-LAN outside at `192.168.1.5`; inside transit to DSW1 | Owns upstream firewall/NAT boundary and exchanges routes with DSW1 |
| SeasonalNet-SW1 (`WS-C2960X-24TS-LL`) | Non-PoE access/aggregation switch | Fiber uplink `Gi0/25` to DSW1 `Gi1/0/12`; retained copper trunk `Gi0/23` to SW2 | Carries CME-R1 and general lab access; no longer the STP root |
| SeasonalNet-SW2 (`WS-C2960X-24PS-L`) | PoE access switch | Fiber uplink `Gi1/0/25` to DSW1; copper trunks `Gi1/0/24` to SW1 and `Gi1/0/22` to SW3 | Fiber is the root path; the SW1 copper path is alternate/blocking, while SW2 is designated toward SW3 |
| SeasonalNet-SW3 (`WS-C2960CX-8PC-L`) | Compact PoE Phone-LAN/access switch | Fiber uplink `Gi0/11` to DSW1; retained copper trunk `Gi0/10` to SW2 | Fiber is the root path; the SW2 copper path is alternate/blocking on SW3 |
| SeasonalCME-R1 (`2901-CME-SRST`)| CME router / UC node | Voice VLAN 10 attachment through SW1 | Dual-stack CME application node; no longer a default gateway |
| SeasonalNet-NP-R1 (`1921/K9`) | Non-production lab router | Main-LAN management at `192.168.1.54` | Former SeasonalEDGE-R1; removed from production VLAN routing, NAT, DHCP, and OSPF |

The DSW1 fiber trunks carry the applicable VLANs from `10,30,40,41,42,43,99`, with VLAN 99 used as the native Main-LAN transit/access VLAN. The legacy copper trunks remain physically connected for redundancy: Rapid-PVST currently blocks SW2 `Gi1/0/24` toward SW1 and SW3 `Gi0/10` toward SW2, leaving the direct DSW1 fiber uplinks as the preferred root paths.

## Known Limitations / Remaining Items

- Migrate SeasonalNet-DSW1, SW1, SW2, SW3, and other general network-device management identities from CME-MGMT/VLAN 42 to NET-MGMT/VLAN 40; keep the current VLAN 42 addresses authoritative until that cutover is complete.
- SeasonalNet-SW1 remains IPv4-only for its current routed management because its LAN Lite feature set does not install a usable off-link IPv6 default-router state in this deployment.
- Keep the disabled Aironet and powerline paths documented as retired/quarantined unless deliberately restored.
- Do not describe the SW1–SW2 or SW2–SW3 copper trunks as unused: they remain connected redundant paths and are held in alternate/blocking state by Rapid-PVST during normal operation.
- Treat DSW1 runtime output as authoritative for current IPv6 RA/DHCPv6 policy until those client-facing details are intentionally standardized.
